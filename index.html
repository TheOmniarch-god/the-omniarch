Sovereign
Immortal Body

Fairy in Red Cape — The House That Never Refuses • Length: 1 ch • 5.2k words
HQ 97 • 1600×700 • Centre cropped expanded

How he got it. Length: 1 chapter, ~5,200 words, 18 min read. After the Battle of Yi Tian Mountain, Lang Ya land spirit refines sovereign immortal fetus Gu — a rank nine Gu that is itself a man. Spectral Soul’s contingency, Shadow Sect’s obsession. In Chapter 1019, Fang Yuan discards his mortal frame and enters. The light detonates. When it clears, there is no blood, no rejection. Only a body that does not fight itself.

The sovereign immortal body holds non-conflicting dao marks. To other immortals, every new mark is a tenant that evicts another; to Fang Yuan, every path is a guest the house welcomes. Strength path does not reject time path, wisdom does not poison blood. This is why later, on Reverse Flow River, he can be dragon and man within breaths — the dao marks never argue.

“My body was created from the rank nine sovereign immortal fetus Gu. It seems that the dao marks in me are not few in number.” — Fang Yuan, musing at first breath

The red cape in the image is not mere costume — fan artists tied it to his incomplete freedom transformation from Reckless Savage’s true inheritance. When the cape lifts and his hand reaches for that stray starlight, he is not praying. He is testing whether heaven still answers.

The Bear
of Jade

Shackled Cage — Yao Le & The Black Bear • Length: 2 ch • 7.1k words
HQ 1805×1016 • ullaseu

The famous bear scene. Length: 2 chapters (151–152), ~7,100 words, 24 min. Gu Yue Yao Le wakes in a cave, ankles tied to rock with vine. Fang Yuan pets the black bear’s fur and says, almost casually, “Because of the wolf tide, it was not easy to find such a wild bear, it took me lots of time and effort to find it.” The art — ullaseu’s blue cave — freezes that instant before the first bite.

She had taken Bear Enslavement Gu from Xiong Jiao Man. He took it back. He uses it to plant hunger where there was hesitation, then forces the beast to swallow intestines, brain, bone shards that cut its own throat. Hunger is not enough. Control must complete what nature refuses.

An hour later, the bear burns from inside. Charcoal Gu, then three Kerosene Gu, then a thousand primeval stones into the white lump that is Man-Beast Life Burial Gu. The membrane forms — round, light, new aperture wall. Fang Yuan, rank three.
