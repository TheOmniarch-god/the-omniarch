Sovereign
Immortal Body

Fairy in Red Cape — The House That Never Refuses
Centre cropped • Expanded • 1200×750 card → 1600×900 hero

How he got it. After the Battle of Yi Tian Mountain, Lang Ya land spirit refines sovereign immortal fetus Gu — a rank nine Gu that is itself a man. Spectral Soul’s contingency, Shadow Sect’s obsession. In Chapter 1019, Fang Yuan discards his mortal frame and enters. The light detonates. When it clears, there is no blood, no rejection. Only a body that does not fight itself.

The sovereign immortal body holds non-conflicting dao marks. To other immortals, every new mark is a tenant that evicts another; to Fang Yuan, every path is a guest the house welcomes. Strength path does not reject time path, wisdom does not poison blood. This is why later, on Reverse Flow River, he can be dragon and man within breaths — the dao marks never argue.

“My body was created from the rank nine sovereign immortal fetus Gu. It seems that the dao marks in me are not few in number.” — Fang Yuan, musing at first breath

The red cape in the image is not mere costume — fan artists tied it to his incomplete freedom transformation from Reckless Savage’s true inheritance. When the cape lifts and his hand reaches for that stray starlight, he is not praying. He is testing whether heaven still answers. It does not. He will answer himself.

Why it matters. Without this body, there is no fate-breaking, no refinement path ascendancy. The novel’s second half is built on this tolerance. If you adore Fang Yuan, you adore this house — open, indifferent, capable of housing everything while belonging to nothing.

The Bear
of Jade

Shackled Cage — Yao Le & The Black Bear
Ch.151 Demonic Nature • Ch.152 Rank Three

The famous bear scene. Gu Yue Yao Le wakes in a cave, ankles tied to rock with vine. Fang Yuan pets the black bear’s fur and says, almost casually, “Because of the wolf tide, it was not easy to find such a wild bear, it took me lots of time and effort to find it.” The art — ullaseu’s blue cave — freezes that instant before the first bite.

She had taken Bear Enslavement Gu from Xiong Jiao Man. He took it back. He uses it to plant hunger where there was hesitation, then — after the throat, after the fresh blood bubbles onto black fur — he forces the beast to swallow intestines, brain, bone shards that cut its own throat. Hunger is not enough. Control must complete what nature refuses.

An hour later, the bear burns from inside. Charcoal Gu, then three Kerosene Gu, then a thousand primeval stones into the white lump that is Man-Beast Life Burial Gu. The membrane forms — round, light, new aperture wall. Fang Yuan, rank three.

Gu Yue Yao Le was shocked when she heard this. Fang Yuan had already struck at lightning speed — and their bodies vanished with Stealth Scale Gu.

Chapters. Chapter 151: Demonic Nature (the cave). Chapter 152: Advance to Rank Three (the refinement). Art by ullaseu — watermark cropped centre, expanded to fill the world. This is the novel’s first unapologetic thesis: Fang Yuan will use anyone to refine Gu, even a girl who thought she was beautiful enough to bargain.

Trail of Blood

Fang Yuan’s Journey — The Path That Bleeds
Blood Path • Persistence, Not Cruelty

Talk about Fang Yuan’s Journey. The green robe is torn, the left eye leaks blood, the world behind him is a red haze of slaughtered structure and slaughtered men. This image — widely shared on TikTok as “the demon’s commute” — is not a single chapter but a synthesis: Qing Mao, Bai Ning Bing, Hu Immortal, Lang Ya, Northern Plains.

Each smear is a lesson: that to walk the demon path is not to enjoy cruelty but to endure it. Gu are essence of heaven and earth, but the essence that sticks longest to Fang Yuan is blood — his own, most of all. He has died to rank six lightning, been frozen, been shredded by sword light, and each time he reconstitutes with Spring Autumn Cicada’s patience.

“The path of demons is persistence, not cruelty.”

Blood talk. Via blood.talk — not a Gu, but a conceit: Fang Yuan talks to blood as if it were a record. Blood path Gu Masters read the past in stains; Fang reads his own failures and refines them. The trail is his diary.

Fate Gu,
Shattered

Threads of Heaven — The Tower Breaks
Ch.1960 Destroying Fate! • Watermark removed

Talk about it. The red cape still there — Fang Yuan has not changed clothes, heaven has changed constraints. Over Heaven Overseeing Tower, fate is not a metaphor but a lattice: crystal threads, visible only to killer move Everybody’s Vision, snaring every life. Star Constellation, Duke Long, Heavenly Court — they guard it because without Fate Gu, ascension is lottery, order is gamble.

Fang Yuan arrives with Spectral Soul, Red Lotus’s will, Reckless Savage’s will, Genesis Lotus. The venerables’ tools — not their power, their wills — lever the threads. In Chapter 1960 — Destroying Fate!, the threads snap. The image’s white lattice across the red cape is that instant: fate shattering into snow, the icy palace below reflecting a path no longer pre-written.

“Humans are the spirit of all living things, Gu are the true essence of heaven and earth — but Fate was the chain.”

The threads on the screen are not filter. They are the Gu world’s former prison. When they dissolve, the famous line from Chapter 1962 drifts across the regions’ sky: “I still think that the fairy in the red cape looks nicer.” The world can finally choose which beauty to praise.

← Back to Library05 • Emerald • Prologue Poem

Emerald
Genesis

Reverend Insanity — Introduction & Poem
Correct translation • ri.theomniarch.com.ng

The line with the poem. On the reverse flow river, ten thousand immortals retreat but love does not. The image’s blue torrent is that river — immortals’ killer moves freeze mid-air as time threads still. Wading through it, Fang Yuan, alone, back to viewer, blue sash snapping.

The poem, as correctly rendered by The Omniarch Translation (see ri.theomniarch.com.ng), reads:

Majestic mountains — the endless path is truly like iron;
Yet today, I stride forward and cross it from the start.
Facing peril, I refine the essence of Heaven and Earth into my body;
My heart, as ever, gazes at the Heavens

Not the common mistranslation that fumbles tense. This keeps Gu Zhen Ren’s register: geography as metaphor (mountains = cultivation walls), “I stride… from the start” = Spring Autumn Cicada’s restart, “refine… into my body” = Gu refinement as ontology, “gazes at the Heavens” = not rebellion but appraisal — heaven as rival craftsman.

The emerald is not just colour — it is the novel’s first promise that all peak fiction is meant to be appreciated. The torrent will try to drown you. Keep walking.

Kodoku
Studio

Fan-Made Manhua — The Face in Comics
Xiaohongshu watermark removed • Centre expanded

Introduction and appreciation of Kodoku Studio. When the novel was removed from Chinese shelves in 2019, the manhua kept it breathing. Kodoku Studio — not official, but more faithful than official — drew Fang Yuan’s hair like ink that refuses to settle, drew Mo Yao’s eyes like cunning itself, drew the Gu as jewelry that bites.

This close-up — two faces, wind-tangled hair, the teal fin-ornaments of a mermaid-like immortal — is Kodoku at peak: no longer adapting Gu Zhen Ren, interpreting him. The jawline’s sharpness, the half-smirk, the way the girl’s fingers barely touch his sleeve as if afraid the demon will turn and test whether she, too, is Gu material.

Fan art is not secondary canon. When the canon is banned, fan art is the canon’s courier.

Surrender
at Dusk

Northern Plain — Fang Yuan Surrenders
Ch.1249–1260 • Golden Clouds

The one that happens in dusk with clouds golden in color and a silhouette in the middle — famous northern plain arc about Fang Yuan surrendering. The sky is a vortex of gold, the ground is cold iron, the army surrounds a single tattered figure whose hair is tangled like threads of fate itself. He does not run.

Check sources: Fan wiki “Surrender — Chapter 1249 Reappearance of Ghost Face / 1258 Fang Yuan versus Five” and TikTok edits tagged #surrender. Cornered by Ying Wu Xie, Shi Nong, and the righteous pursuit after Hu Immortal’s fall, Fang Yuan — now sword dragon — cannot win. So he offers capture. In Gu world, surrender is not humiliation but a killer move: it buys time, it lets the enemy carry your will into their grotto-heaven.

The clouds were golden at dusk, as if heaven itself had been refined into a Gu and was weeping light.

The silhouette in the image is that choice: arms loose, cape torn, facing a sun that is also a Gu formation’s eye. He surrenders to Lang Ya later, to Heavenly Court later — each surrender a seed for later betrayal. The arc is beloved because it proves Fang Yuan’s doctrine: the only shame is to die without cost.
